# Framework v3.3.1

Released on 2026-08-31.

## Framework Changes

- Re-toned the map's area fills so water reads as water. Every token from `gray-60` up dithers
  only the top two of the four levels a 2-bit panel has, so a sea sat 10 of 255 from a forest and
  a coastline read as an outline rather than a massing; on 1-bit the two were one Bayer tile at
  nested densities, four cells in sixty-four apart. The fills are spaced on the 1-bit rail now,
  whose 12.5 ink points is the smallest step that separates. `map-water` moves `gray-60` →
  `gray-45`, and to `checker` on the 1-bit rail; `map-green` moves `gray-65` → `gray-60`;
  `map-water-line` `gray-40` → `gray-30`; `map-road-minor` `gray-50` → `gray-45`; `map-path`
  `gray-45` → `gray-55`, where a dashed line never renders its density.
- Added the `map-forest` and `map-rock` slots. One tone carried all twenty-two vegetated kinds,
  and bare rock and scree painted as beach sand, so an alpine valley arrived as a single flat
  field. Woodland draws with the green group and rock with the sand group, so no preset gains a
  flag.
- Widened the contrast casing markers and routes already carried, so they hold against a busy
  map. `TRMNLMaps.route()` draws 4 CSS pixels with 2 of casing a side (an 8 pixel band, was 5);
  `TRMNLMaps.dot()` draws radius 5 with a 2 pixel ring (14 pixels across, was 10). Style lines —
  roads, rail, water — are unchanged.
- Moved the dark theme onto a strict mirror of the base ramp, which its own comment already
  claimed. `map-building` moves `gray-45` → `gray-20` as a consequence.

## Upgrade Notes

- **A theme that overrides `map-green` no longer covers woodland, and one that overrides
  `map-sand` no longer covers bare rock.** CSS custom properties cannot make this degrade
  gracefully — a slot that referenced another would always equal it — so add `map-forest` beside
  your `map-green` and `map-rock` beside your `map-sand`. Both are named in the theme
  boilerplate.
- The black-and-yellow and white-and-red themes keep their previous water tone. Their ramps are
  too compressed for the step: on the yellow canvas one shade lighter prints as the woodland
  tone, and on the red canvas the fifty-point tone is a gray checkerboard that would drop the hue
  on a color panel.
- `route()` and `dot()` draw thicker than they did. Pass an explicit `width` or `radius` to keep
  the previous sizes.

## Documentation Changes

- Moved the Map styles example to zoom 14. Buildings, sites and paths are gated at minzoom 14 and
  minor roads, street areas and transit at 13, so below 13 the `streets` and `minimal` presets
  render the same picture and the example taught nothing. The section now names the zooms detail
  starts at.
- Relocated the Map examples: streets and the floating card to Atlanta, styles to Berlin, the
  route to a walk through Tiergarten, and the Strava activity to Central Park. The floating card
  gained the layout it was missing, which had put its label and its value on one line.
- Corrected the Strava example's stats against its own route, and gave the maps the rounded
  corner plugins already use.
