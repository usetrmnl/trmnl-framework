# Framework v3.4.4

Released on 2026-10-24.

## Framework Changes

- On TRMNL color screens, label and `bg--*` text is chosen from the colors the panel actually shows, not from pure red, yellow, green and blue. Red labels and backgrounds now carry white text on 4-color, Spectra 6 and 7-color screens, and green carries white text on Spectra 6. Grayscale, 2-bit and 3-color screens are unchanged.

## Upgrade Notes

- Plugins pinned to 3.4.3 keep black text on red until they move to 3.4.4. To keep a specific color, add a `text--*` class to an inner element.

## Documentation Changes

- The Spacing page says margin and padding take Fixed Sizes only, and links to the Fixed Sizes table.
